Tracé

An Android and Android TV app from MA Soft that turns a shared Google Maps link (a place, a route or a saved list) into a travel itinerary: landmarks, photo spots and food stops just off your route.

Public APIs this app uses

  • Wikipedia (MediaWiki Action API): nearby articles (geosearch), short descriptions, lead images and intro extracts. Each request identifies the app as Trace/<version> with a link to this page.
  • Google Places API and Routes API: only when a user adds their own API key in the app's settings.

How the app keeps its traffic light

  • At most 2 requests at a time to Wikipedia, about 50 per trip, made once when a trip is created.
  • Results are cached on the device, so reopening a trip makes no network requests.
  • It honours HTTP 429 and Retry-After, and retries missed requests later instead of repeating them right away.
  • No AI or machine-learning services are used. All matching and ranking runs on the device with fixed rules.
